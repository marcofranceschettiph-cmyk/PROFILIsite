The press release

The festival’s official presentation text, by IANUA.

The IANUA artistic collective presents PRO-FILI, Edition 0, part of the Municipality’s Ottobre Rodigino programme and conceived as a short choral journey in which the audience does not merely watch the events but enters into a direct relationship with local artists.

The thread becomes a conceptual driving force, drawing emerging artists closer to the audience they may have tomorrow, and drawing the public closer to kinds of art it may not yet know.

The soul of PRO-FILI

  • WorkshopsImmersive workshops for all ages, open to the whole community.
  • Local artistsShowcasing artists from the Rovigo area and their current work.
  • GuestsFour special guests alongside the local artists: the painter Massimo Marchioro, the singer-songwriter FILO, the visual artist CAINO and the musician NIHIL.
  • The thread as a networkA moment of direct, informal encounter between the community and the artists, strengthening the network between local cultural organisations, the media and the public.
  • ProgrammeA varied programme: workshops, music, photography, installation, dialogue.
  • Art and responsibilityReal relationships between people, in which art is both a bond and a responsibility.
