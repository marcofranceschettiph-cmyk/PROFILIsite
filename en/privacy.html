Who processes the data

PRO-FILI Festival is conceived and curated by IANUA, an artistic collective from Rovigo that is in the process of being established as an association.

Until the association is established, the data controller is Marco Franceschetti, acting on behalf of the collective. For questions or requests about your data, write to ianuaeventi@gmail.com.

Once the association is established, we will update this page with its details.

What data and why

When you write to us via the contact form

You give us your name, email and message, and we use them only to reply to you. The form opens your email program with the message already written: it reaches us as an ordinary email. We delete messages within 12 months of the last exchange, unless a collaboration has arisen that requires us to keep them.

When you write to us by email

We use the data you give us to reply to you, with the same retention periods as for the contact form. Our email is on Gmail, a free account, so messages also pass through Google’s servers.

If you apply as an artist

Through the application form we collect the data needed to assess your proposal and, if it is selected, to organise your participation in the festival: name, contact details, presentation, images of your work.

We publish your name, biography and photo on the website only if you give your consent in the form. After the festival, your page remains online in the archive of past editions.

You can withdraw your consent at any time by writing to ianuaeventi@gmail.com: within 30 days we will remove all references to you from the website, namely your name, photo, biography and personal page. Anything published before then remains lawful.

The form is managed with Google Forms, on a free Google account, so the data is also stored by Google. Applications that are not selected are deleted at the end of the festival edition.

If you propose a partnership or sponsorship

Through the partner and sponsor form we collect the data needed to assess and organise the collaboration: name, organisation, contact details, proposal. We use it only for this purpose and keep it for as long as the collaboration is active or under negotiation.

This form, too, is managed with Google Forms, on a free Google account, so the data is also stored by Google.

Cookies

The website does not use cookies or any other tools that store information on your device, does not use analytics or tracking systems, and does not embed third-party content in its pages. That is why you will not find any cookie notice.

Fonts are loaded from the website itself. If cookies, analytics or embedded content are added to the website in future, we will update this page before enabling them.

Who receives the data

We do not sell or pass on your data. It is processed on our behalf only by the services we use for the website and for contacts:

  • GitHub (GitHub Pages), which hosts the website: for security purposes it logs the IP address of visitors.
  • Google (Gmail and Google Forms, on a free account), for email and for the artist and partner/sponsor application forms.

Google and GitHub may also process data in the United States, under the safeguards of the EU-US Data Privacy Framework: their own privacy policies apply (Google, GitHub).

Your rights

You can ask us at any time to access the data we hold about you, to correct it, to erase it, to restrict its use or to receive it in a readable format. You can also object to processing based on our legitimate interest and withdraw any consent you have given us.

Write to ianuaeventi@gmail.com: we will reply within one month. If you believe your data is being processed unlawfully, you can lodge a complaint with the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali (the Italian Data Protection Authority).

We do not make automated decisions about people and do not create profiles.
