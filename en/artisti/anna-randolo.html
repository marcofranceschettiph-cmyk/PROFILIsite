The biography

A children’s illustrator, born in 1997, with a diploma in graphic design and comics. She wrote and illustrated «Il Regno del Sottosuolo» (The Underground Kingdom), her first picture book, now looking for a publisher. Over the years she has worked with very different clients, from clothing brands to La Voce di Rovigo, from Delta Radio to language schools, always with a single common thread: giving colour and voice to stories.
