Biography

I have always used creativity and the senses to explore things I could not discover any other way. What does learning to recognise beauty teach us? Is there an emotional fabric in which a dissonant feeling can change its tone and meaning? What lies behind certain images that seem to come from elsewhere, from a dream or a past outside time? As a child it was a game of perception and imagination; for some years now it has also been my work, mostly through images. I have no answers, only impressions. So I keep trying.
