The biography

I am a multidisciplinary artist: dancer, pianist, actress and educator. I move between music, dance, theatre and the visual arts, creating connections between languages and people. I love turning experiences and cross-pollinations into projects that are authentic, creative and alive.

She is part of the IANUA collective, which organises the festival: she looks after its communication and its Bolzano-Merano hub.
