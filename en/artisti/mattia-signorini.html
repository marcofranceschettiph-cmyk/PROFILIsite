Biography

Mattia Signorini was born in 1980 and lives in Rovigo. His novels include «La sinfonia del tempo breve» (Salani 2009, Marsilio 2013), «Le fragili attese» (Marsilio 2015) and «Una piccola pace» (Feltrinelli 2022, Universale Economica 2025). His books have been translated in England, France, Germany, Spain, the Netherlands, South America and the Arab world.
