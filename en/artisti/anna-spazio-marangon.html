The biography

Anna Spazio Marangon is an art teacher and artist. She has taken part in exhibitions including Boot in Düsseldorf and Modus Operandi, and in art residencies at Islington Mill (Manchester) and PalauArte. She worked on the 54th Venice Art Biennale. Her artistic research focuses on the glitch in contemporary communication between machine and human, and on the constant need for social links.
