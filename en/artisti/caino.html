The biography

CAINO was born in the late 1980s in a small village in Umbria, where creativity found room among books and pencils. From childhood, drawing was his refuge, but life took him elsewhere. He graduated with top marks from a technical institute and started working early, sacrificing his artistic nature for stability.

And yet his passion for cinema fed a love of telling stories through images. In 2019 he left his job and began to travel, discovering forgotten places and stories that deserve to be told. Then a dramatic event — an ischaemia and heart surgery — taught him the value of time. From that moment on, photography became his language.
