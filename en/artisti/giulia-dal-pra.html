The biography

I collect things that others have stopped looking at: a word met by chance, a photograph forgotten in a magazine, a page torn from an old book, a face, a colour. I started making collage in 2020, when I felt the need to give shape to ideas; what began almost as a game became a path, with competitions and exhibitions. I like taking something that already exists and giving it a new chance: I move, pair and recompose fragments, looking for connections where there seem to be none. For me paper is memory, image, word: it has already lived before it reaches my hands.
