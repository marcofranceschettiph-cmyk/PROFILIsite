The biography

I am an artist from the Polesine, versatile and gifted with an inexhaustible curiosity bent on uncovering the secrets of matter. Since I began painting in 2003, I have been endlessly drawn to the techniques and forms of applied art, working with wood, glass, metal, Plexiglas, semi-precious stones, fabrics and coloured pigments.

It is the exploration of materials, sometimes humble or reclaimed ones, that fires my passion and my innate desire to alter and assemble them in a very personal style, following the teachings of the great craftsmen of my land. I start from the idea that every material holds its own aesthetic dignity and that, in the alchemy of varied combinations, extraordinary impressions can arise and, at times, forms of artistic provocation.

The protagonist of my work is resin, fluid and mysterious in its make-up, hard to master completely. Resin creates an ephemeral, illusory beauty; it captures the instant and crystallises it, rising to become a symbol of the human condition, conflicted and tormented by the obsession with consumption.

In 2008 I took on experimentation with mixed-material indoor installations and, soon after, created installations in parks and gardens. In 2010, after a careful selection, I was entrusted with the installation project for the park of Casa Malipiero, «Arte e scenografia in parchi e giardini»: I stayed in that celebrated village for five years, in a constant process of inner growth.

Since 2015 I have worked with companies on site-specific indoor and outdoor projects, putting my natural empathy at their service, with clients and places alike; that same year I moved to Venice, where I carried out major projects. At the end of 2019 I returned to the Polesine, and the following year I took on heavy metals, iron and steel, which became an integral part of my research and of my indoor and outdoor works.

I have always let myself be seduced by the nature around me: the horizon of the vast countryside becomes a source of inspiration and of complete expressive freedom. In the same way, wild nature, where everything seems pure and untamed, draws my gaze, which catches life and movement with attentive passion and makes them its own.
