The biography

What does a headdress tell? This question is where Martina Barison’s path begins: she conceives and produces exhibitions and interdisciplinary events around the private collection «Da ogni capo del mondo», weaving together visual art, storytelling, music and performance.

Travel, both physical and conceptual, is her signature: the headdress becomes an anthropological and aesthetic key to reading the world, for exhibitions and educational paths designed for different audiences. She lives in Lusia, in the province of Rovigo.
