Biography

Maryam Amirfarshi is an Iranian multidisciplinary visual artist. She graduated in Visual Arts in Bologna and lives in Italy. Through painting, mixed media and poetic research she develops an expressive language that weaves together symbols, memory, identity and transformation.

Her works explore the fragility of the contemporary human condition, creating a dialogue between myth, reality and imagination. She lives and works in Rovigo.
